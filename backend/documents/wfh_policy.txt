Work From Home Policy

1. Eligibility
Employees in good standing, who have completed their probation period, are eligible to work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Employees still in probation are generally expected to work from the office to support onboarding and team integration.

2. Request Process
Work-from-home requests must be raised at least 1 working day in advance via the internal WFH request system. Recurring WFH schedules (e.g., every Monday and Friday) can be approved in advance for a full quarter.

3. Availability Expectations
Employees working from home are expected to:
- Be available on company communication tools (chat, video calls) during standard working hours (9:30 AM to 6:30 PM).
- Attend all scheduled meetings with video enabled where required.
- Maintain the same productivity standards as when working from the office.

4. Equipment and Security
Employees working from home must use company-approved devices and connect via the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
Sharing office laptops or credentials with family members or others is strictly prohibited.

5. Full-Time Remote Work
Full-time work from home (5 days a week) is only permitted under special circumstances, such as documented medical conditions or approved relocation, and requires sign-off from both the reporting manager and HR.

6. Exceptions and Suspension of Privileges
The company reserves the right to temporarily suspend WFH privileges for an employee or team if performance concerns arise or if business needs require in-office presence (e.g., client visits, audits).
