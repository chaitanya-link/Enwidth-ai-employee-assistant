Employee Benefits

1. Health Insurance
The company provides group health insurance coverage for employees and their immediate family members (spouse and up to 2 children), covering hospitalization expenses up to INR 5,00,000 per year.

2. Wellness Allowance
Employees are eligible for an annual wellness allowance of INR 10,000, which can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, or annual health checkups. This amount must be claimed with valid bills through the HR portal.

3. Provident Fund and Gratuity
The company contributes to the Employee Provident Fund (EPF) as per statutory guidelines. Employees who complete 5 or more years of continuous service are also eligible for gratuity as per the Payment of Gratuity Act.

4. Employee Stock Options (ESOPs)
Select employees, based on role and tenure, may be granted Employee Stock Options as part of their compensation, vesting over a 4-year period with a 1-year cliff.

5. Meal Vouchers
Employees working from the office receive daily meal vouchers, credited monthly, to be used at the office cafeteria or partner outlets.

6. Maternity and Childcare Benefits
In addition to maternity leave, the company provides a one-time childcare allowance and flexible working hours for the first 6 months after an employee returns from maternity leave.

7. Learning and Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 15,000 for courses, certifications, or conference attendance, subject to manager approval.

8. What Is Not Covered
For clarity, the following are NOT currently provided as part of company benefits:
- Pet insurance
- Dental insurance
- Vision/eyewear reimbursement
- Personal vehicle maintenance allowance
