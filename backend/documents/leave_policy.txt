Leave Policy

1. Types of Leave
The company provides the following categories of leave to all full-time employees:
- Casual Leave (CL): 6 days per year, used for short personal matters.
- Sick Leave (SL): 6 days per year, used for health-related absences.
- Earned Leave (EL): 12 days per year, accrued monthly and used for planned time off.
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of paid leave for female employees, as per statutory guidelines.
- Paternity Leave: 10 working days of paid leave for male employees following the birth or adoption of a child.
- Unpaid Leave: May be granted at management discretion once all paid leave categories are exhausted.

2. Total Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to a total of 18 paid leaves per calendar year (6 Casual + 6 Sick + 12 Earned, with Earned Leave accruing at 1 day per month).

3. Leave Application Process
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 2 working days in advance, except in genuine medical emergencies, where retrospective approval may be granted.
Each request requires manager approval before it is considered confirmed. Employees should not assume approval until they receive confirmation.

4. Leave Balance and Carry Forward
Unused Earned Leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any Earned Leave beyond this cap will lapse at year-end.
Casual Leave and Sick Leave cannot be carried forward and will lapse if unused.

5. Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays annually, as per the calendar published by HR at the start of each year. Public holidays are separate from the leave balance described above.

6. Leave During Notice Period
Employees serving their notice period may take leave only with explicit written approval from their manager and HR, subject to business requirements.
