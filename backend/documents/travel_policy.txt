Travel Policy

1. Eligibility and Approval
Employees traveling for official work purposes are eligible for reimbursement of travel, accommodation, and meal expenses, provided the trip is pre-approved by the reporting manager before any booking is made.
Unapproved travel expenses will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.

2. Booking Process
All domestic and international travel must be booked through the company's approved travel desk or booking portal. Employees should not book personal travel agents unless the travel desk is unavailable, in which case prior written approval is required.

3. Class of Travel
- Domestic flights: Economy class is standard for all employees.
- International flights: Economy class for flights under 6 hours; Premium Economy for flights over 6 hours, subject to approval.
- Business class travel requires VP-level approval and is reserved for senior leadership or exceptional circumstances.

4. Accommodation
Employees are entitled to stay in 3-star or 4-star hotels for domestic travel and up to 4-star hotels for international travel, within the per-night budget cap published by Finance each year.

5. Per Diem and Meal Allowance
A daily per diem is provided to cover meals and incidental expenses while traveling, at rates that vary by city tier (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3) and country, as published in the Finance handbook.

6. Reimbursement Claims
All reimbursement claims must be submitted within 15 days of completing the trip, along with valid receipts and a filled expense report. Claims submitted after 30 days will require additional approval from Finance and may be rejected.

7. Safety Guidelines
Employees traveling internationally must inform HR of their itinerary in advance and register with the company's travel safety partner, where applicable, for emergency assistance.
